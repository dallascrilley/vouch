# Vouch brand

**Formerly:** Quorum  
**Mark:** Top-down fist-bump — cream robot fist + diverse human fists, sage scalloped check seal  
**Vibe:** Wholesome, collaborative, human-in-the-loop trust  
**Tagline:** Human review as an API.

## Naming

- Product name: **Vouch**
- Repo may still be `quorum` until a rename; user-facing copy should say Vouch.
- Avoid "quorum" in new marketing copy except migration notes.

## Do / don't

- **Do** keep the robot as peer in the huddle (not a servant, not a villain).
- **Do** keep the sage check seal as the "approved / consensus" cue.
- **Don't** use cold corporate blues or heavy black metal aesthetics.
- **Don't** drop the humans — the product is human review.
